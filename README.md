# Spatial-Assessment-of-Onshore-Wind-Capacity-in-UK
This code identifies 20 optimal locations for 0.5 GW wind farms, to- taling 10 GW of capacity. 17 sites are situated in Scotland, two in Wales, and one in Northern Ireland.
Due to the significant spatial footprint required and stringent environmental and land-use constraints, suitable locations could not be identified within England. While quotas aimed for a balanced regional and grid-supporting distribution, the fallback methodology ultimately concentrated development in high-potential Scottish regions. For the suitability analysis, a Suitability Index map was developed using the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to balance environmental protection, technical feasibility, and social acceptance. A geometry-based algorithm ensured the optimal positioning of 81 turbines per site across a 125 km2 area to mitigate wake effects. Finally, a financial analysis confirms that the projects are economically robust and viable without substantial government subsidies. Overall, the results demonstrate that the proposed 10 GW expansion is technically and financially feasible, but may put more pressure on the electricity grid due to the concentration in Scotland.
